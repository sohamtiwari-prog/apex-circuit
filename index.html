iPhone and iPad

You're on an iPhone or iPad: this one's for you.

Two ways in. Start with the browser: nothing to install.

1. Play in Safari

Play in the browser
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Play in the browser. The first load is about 45 MB; after that it starts quickly.
  2. For full screen: tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen, and play from the Apex Circuit icon from then on.
  3. Typing your name or a code: tap the box and a text bar opens at the top, above the keyboard. Tap Done when you're finished.
  4. Tilt steering: allow motion when asked (on your first tap). Graphics start on Battery saver; Pause → Graphics for High.

2. Install the iPhone app

The app looks and plays exactly like the Android one: full graphics, Wi-Fi races. It's free and isn't on the App Store, so it's installed with SideStore (or AltStore) using your own Apple ID.

Already have SideStore or AltStore? One tap adds Apex Circuit; new versions then show up there as updates.

Add to SideStore Add to AltStore If the button does nothing: in SideStore or AltStore open Sources, tap + and paste https://sohamtiwari-prog.github.io/apex-circuit/apps.json

First time?

  1. Set up SideStore once, following the guide at sidestore.io. It needs a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) for about 15 minutes, once.
  2. Then tap Add to SideStore above and install Apex Circuit from it.
  3. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID, Trust. On iOS 16 or later also turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode (the phone restarts).
  4. A free Apple ID signs apps for 7 days. SideStore renews it on the phone itself (open it once a week, or let its shortcut do it): no computer after the first time. Updates keep your progress.
No SideStore? Download the .ipa and install it from a computer with Sideloadly (sideloadly.io). That works too, but the app has to be re-installed from the computer every 7 days.
To carry your progress over from the browser or another phone: Your driver → Backup code, then Restore in the app.

Computers

Play in the browser Arrows or WASD to drive, Space to brake, C for the camera, R to reset, Esc to pause.

Android

You're on Android: get the app.

Download for Android (30.1 MB) Or get it from the GitHub release
  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Tap Install. It updates over your current version and keeps your progress.
Always the newest build. Progress in the browser version is kept in that browser.
